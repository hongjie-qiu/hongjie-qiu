## 1. Headline numbers for Q3 FY2027 (quarter ended Oct 25, 2026; reported after the close on Tue Nov 17, 2026), comparison with consensus, one-off items

### Takeaway
Q3 FY2027 revenue was a record $115.8B (+103% YoY, +20% QoQ) with GAAP/non-GAAP gross margin of 74.8%/74.9%, GAAP operating income $77.5B, GAAP net income $65.3B and diluted EPS of $2.70 GAAP / $2.68 non-GAAP — a beat of roughly 6% on revenue (consensus ≈ $109B) and 8% on EPS (consensus ≈ $2.48), and ~7% above the company's own $108.0B ±2% guide. One-offs were small this quarter (net equity-investment gain $1.8B including a $1.8B impairment of private stakes) versus much larger investment gains in Q1–Q2 FY2027; no inventory or export-related charge was found.

## 2. Revenue by market platform (latest quarter and prior 4–5 quarters), product generation, China, customer concentration

Since Q1 FY2027 Nvidia reports only two market platforms — Data Center (sub-markets Hyperscale and "ACIE" = AI Clouds, Industrial & Enterprise) and Edge Computing — so Gaming, Professional Visualization, Automotive and OEM are no longer disclosed separately. - Management's tone on customer financing has shifted from expansion to selectivity (conditional neocloud commitments, "rigorously evaluating"), which lowers incremental balance-sheet risk but could also remove a demand support for capital-constrained neoclouds.
