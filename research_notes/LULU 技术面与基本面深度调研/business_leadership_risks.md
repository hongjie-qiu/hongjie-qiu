## 1. Demand and brand health in the Americas

### Takeaway
Every hard data point found through September 2026 shows Americas demand still deteriorating, not stabilizing: Americas comparable sales went from -5% (fiscal Q1) to -12% (fiscal Q2), leggings sales fell about 20%, markdowns rose, third-party data shows share loss to Alo and Vuori, and management guided fiscal Q3 revenue down 10–11%. The bull evidence is thin and mostly forward-looking (more newness in the assortment, website visits up, "green shoots" in looser-fit bottoms, a guide that the CFO said reflects current trend). No independent demand data for October–November 2026 was found.

Third-party data consistently shows lululemon ceding share to Alo and Vuori (from small bases) while On's apparel is growing ~39% and teens are rotating away from athletic brands in general; however, at least one direct competitor (Athleta, comps -12%) is doing as badly, so part of the weakness is category-wide in premium women's activewear. ## 3. International (China Mainland and Rest of World)

### Takeaway
International growth has slowed sharply and no longer offsets the Americas: China Mainland revenue growth fell to +4% reported (-2% constant currency) in fiscal Q2 2026 with comps -2% (-8% constant dollar), and international comps overall turned negative (-3%). Two brand controversies in China (April and June 2026) coincide with the slowdown, and Alo is entering the market.

## 6. External risks (tariffs, de minimis, consumer, FX, litigation)

### Takeaway
Tariffs remain a structural margin headwind despite a one-off IEEPA refund that flattered fiscal Q2 2026 (560 bps of gross margin; $0.86 of EPS); about $105 million of further refunds is possible but excluded from guidance, the de minimis exemption is gone for good, and replacement tariffs of roughly 10–20% still apply. The US consumer backdrop into holiday 2026 is mixed-to-negative for discretionary apparel: clothing-store sales fell 0.7% in October, inflation is elevated, and the Fed is leaning toward hikes. Litigation is a nuisance-level risk; the Texas PFAS probe closed with no PFAS found.
