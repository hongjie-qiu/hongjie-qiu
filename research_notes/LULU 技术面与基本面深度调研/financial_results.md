## 1. Headline numbers for the latest quarter (Q2 FY2026, quarter ended August 2, 2026, reported September 3, 2026) versus consensus and guidance; one-offs and "clean" figures

### Takeaway
Confirmed: the latest reported quarter is Q2 fiscal 2026 (13 weeks ended August 2, 2026), reported after the close on Thursday, September 3, 2026. Revenue fell 4% to $2.416bn (the first year-over-year decline in the series reviewed) and missed both consensus (~$2.46bn) and the company's own guide ($2.450–2.475bn); reported EPS of $2.92 was flattered by $0.86 of IEEPA tariff refunds — ex-refund EPS was about $2.06 (vs $3.10 a year earlier, vs guide $1.76–1.81 and consensus ~$1.79–1.80), ex-refund gross margin about 54.9% (−360bp) and ex-refund operating margin about 13.2% (−750bp).

### Inferences
- Derived: actual Q2 revenue of $2,415.6m was ~$34m (1.4%) below the LOW end of the company's own $2.450bn guide; ex-refund EPS of $2.06 was $0.25–0.30 above the guide range of $1.76–1.81. The EPS "beat" on a clean basis therefore came from margin/cost items (gross margin 50bp better than guided incl. a 40bp accrual reversal, lower share count, slightly lower tax rate), not from sales.
- Underlying earnings power fell far more than the headline: clean EPS −34% y/y and clean operating margin ~13.2% vs 20.7% a year earlier, on a 4% revenue decline — heavy operating deleverage (SG&A +400bp).
- The refund is non-recurring; up to roughly $95–105m more could arrive but is not in guidance, so any further refund would be an "upside" one-off rather than an operating improvement.

## 2. Segment and channel detail for the latest quarter and the trend over the previous 4–6 quarters

### Takeaway
The Americas (about two-thirds of revenue) has moved from low-single-digit declines to −8% revenue / −12% comps in Q2 FY2026, and the international engine that had been offsetting it has stalled: China Mainland revenue growth slowed from +46% (Q3 FY2025) to +28% (Q4), +30% (Q1 FY2026) and then +4% reported / −2% constant currency in Q2 FY2026, with China comps negative. Weakness is broad across channels (stores and digital each down ~6%) and categories (women's −4%, men's ~−1%, accessories −13%, women's leggings ~−20%), and is traffic-led.
