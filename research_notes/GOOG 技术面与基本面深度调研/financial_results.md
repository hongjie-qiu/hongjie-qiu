## 1. Latest quarter headline numbers, comparison with consensus, one-off items and "clean" figures

### Takeaway
Q3 2026 (reported Oct 28, 2026) showed revenue of $127.58B (+25% YoY, reported and constant currency) and operating income of $40.58B (+30%, 31.8% margin), but GAAP diluted EPS fell to $2.12 from $2.87 because of an $8.7B net loss on equity securities (largely SpaceX); even the ex-item EPS of $2.67 missed the roughly $3.02 LSEG consensus, revenue slightly missed (about $128.2B expected), and the shares fell about 10–11% in after-hours / pre-market trading. The "+30%" operating income growth is flattered by a $3.5B EC fine in the Q3 2025 base; on a like-for-like basis operating income grew about 17% and the margin fell about 2 points (see Inferences).

### Inferences
- **"Clean" EPS, Q3 2026:** $2.12 + $0.55 = **$2.67** (matches CNBC's adjusted figure). Versus LSEG $3.02: GAAP miss $0.90 (–30%), ex-item miss $0.35 (about –12%). The Zacks miss of 27.89% implies a Zacks consensus near $2.94 ($2.12 ÷ 0.7211).
- **"Clean" operating income growth, Q3 2026:** Q3 2025 operating income ex-fine ≈ $31.23B + $3.46B = $34.69B. Q3 2026 $40.58B ÷ $34.69B = **about +17%**, not +30%; margin 31.8% vs. 33.9% ex-fine = **about –2.1 points YoY** (Q3 2026 itself still contains unquantified legal charges).
- **Sequential margin compression:** operating income was flat-to-down QoQ ($40.8B in Q2 → $40.58B in Q3) while revenue rose $7.8B; margin went 36.1% (Q1) → 34% (Q2) → 31.8% (Q3).
- **Clean Q1 2026 EPS:** $5.11 – $2.35 = about $2.76.
- **FY2025:** operating margin ≈ 32.0% ($129.04B ÷ $402.8B, derived). Note the FY2025 operating income, net income and EPS figures in the table come from third-party aggregators (StockTitan and similar), not from a direct read of the 10-K; they are mutually consistent but should be treated as medium confidence.
- **TTM to Sep 30, 2026 (derived):** revenue ≈ $471.1B ($113.8B + $109.9B + $119.8B + $127.58B); operating income ≈ $157.0B ($35.9B + $39.7B + $40.8B + $40.58B), a TTM operating margin of about 33.3%.
- **Net income $26.5B vs. $26.2B:** the gap matches quarterly preferred dividends (about 19.25M mandatory convertible preferred shares × $15.63 ≈ $0.30B), so $26.5B is probably net income and $26.2B net income available to common.
- EPS is now a poor quality signal for Alphabet: quarterly GAAP EPS swung $2.82 → $5.11 → $9.11 → $2.12 over four quarters almost entirely because of marks on SpaceX and a private AI holding; operating income and segment results are the stable measures.
