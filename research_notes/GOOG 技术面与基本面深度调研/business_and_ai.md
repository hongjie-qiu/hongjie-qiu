## 1. Search: is generative AI taking query volume or advertising share from Google?

### Takeaway
Query volume is not visibly being lost (management says query growth is at a five-year high and Statcounter still shows roughly 90–91% share), but Search revenue growth slowed sharply to +12% in Q3 2026 from +17% and +19% in the two prior quarters, paid-click growth fell to +8%, and management conceded that AI Mode queries monetize at a lower rate. The pressure that shows up in the numbers is monetization per query, not a measurable loss of queries or ad budgets to ChatGPT.

### Inferences
- Paid-click growth fell 5 points quarter on quarter (13% to 8%) while CPC growth held (3% to 4%). The slowdown is in the number of paid clicks, which fits management's statement that fast-growing AI Mode queries carry fewer ads, on top of the FX drag.
- With query growth at a five-year high and revenue growth at +12%, revenue per query is falling. The bull case needs AI Mode ad load to catch up; the bear case is that conversational answers structurally carry fewer paid clicks.
- Third-party traffic data do not show Google losing absolute volume: google.com visits grew faster than chatgpt.com in October 2026 and Statcounter share sits around 90–91%. These tools measure website visits and referrals, so they undercount in-app and in-chat activity.
- OpenAI's "one-fifth of Google" is self-reported and depends on its own definition of a search; independent estimates in 2026 range from 7.5% to 12%.
- One search summary quoted "$56,567 million" as Q3 2026 Search revenue from the 10-Q table. That is almost certainly the prior-year column: $63.35B divided by $56.567B is 1.12, matching the reported +12%.
- The Safari default is less secure than before (non-exclusive, 12-month terms), while the Gemini–Siri deal ties Apple closer to Google's models. The two effects point in opposite directions.

## 2. Gemini: model generation, ratings, usage and adoption

### Takeaway
Google shipped the Gemini 4 generation in Q4 2026 (Argon on September 30/October 13, then Carbon, Neon and Hydrogen) and usage is very large (Gemini app 1B+ monthly users, about 32 billion API tokens a minute). Model leadership is contested and changes within weeks: the latest third-party index readings put Anthropic's and OpenAI's newest models slightly ahead of Google's best, and Gemini's share of AI website traffic has slipped since July.
